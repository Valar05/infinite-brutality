# Hanging Gardens Nook Story Pilot

Date: 2026-06-11
Status: first concrete district writing pilot

## Purpose

This is the first concrete absent-people story pilot for Infinite Brutality's `hanging_gardens` world family.

It takes the general nook-writing system and collapses it into one playable district-story slice:

- one hidden run-story state
- one district role
- 12 concrete nook packets
- five nook families only
- repeated motifs and contradiction hooks

The target feeling is that the player is moving through a floating or hanging garden settlement after the people have been forced into secrecy, rationing, and improvised rites.

## Chosen Hidden Run Story State

This pilot uses:

- origin population: `terrace_households`
- secondary influence: `rope_market_carriers`
- rupture event: `aqueduct poisoning`
- survival doctrine: `concealment and rationed speech`
- taboo: `do not name the dead after dusk`
- hope: `eastward ascent to the lit terraces`

This is intentionally domestic and infrastructural rather than royal or mythic. The grandeur should come from the architecture and the accumulation of ritual, not from named heroes.

## District Role

`intake_terraces_and_bridge_customs`

This district should read as:

- the place where arrivals were sorted and weighed
- a water and toll interface where official counting used to happen
- a household survival zone carved into that official architecture after the system broke

So the district contains tension between:

- public rule and private survival
- toll logic and household logic
- water infrastructure and poisoned water memory
- route enforcement and secret eastward escape signs

## Packet Coverage

The 12 packets are spread across:

- 4 shelter packets
- 2 shrine packets
- 2 repair packets
- 2 ration packets
- 2 burial packets

That is not a final distribution rule. It is just enough to prove variety while keeping the district readable.

## Repeated Motifs

The pilot intentionally reuses a small motif set:

- blue cord as secret-safe-route marker
- seven-bowl household sharing sets
- eastward directional arrows
- veiled names with visible ages
- water measures reused as ration or child-growth marks

A player should begin recognizing these as cultural signatures rather than one-off props.

## Route-Level Story Arc

If the nooks are encountered in a rough traversal order, the district story should unfold like this:

1. Families are hiding inside water and intake infrastructure.
2. They keep silence as a survival discipline.
3. Carriers and repair workers maintain covert passage routes.
4. Official weights and toll law no longer match real human need.
5. The poison event changed burial and naming behavior.
6. Children are taught the route before they are taught the explanation.
7. A secret eastward escape remains possible, but never for everyone at once.

This is the desired fragmented story arc, not mandatory room order.

# Hanging Gardens Texture Prompts

Date: 2026-06-10

Use these prompts to extend the project-owned vector material family for the new prop pass.

## Global constraints

- low-poly hard-edged look
- flat fills and broad material rhythm
- readable on phone
- no photoreal rendering
- no modern fabric or building patterns
- no text, sigils, or lettering
- no purple fantasy drift

## New textures

### `ib-vector-timber-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly dark fantasy timber texture for hanging market decks, ladders, scaffold rails, trellises, and stalls; broad plank rhythm, split grain, old nail scars, smoke-darkened edges, sun-faded centers, no photoreal bark, no modern wood floor pattern, no text. Flat vector-friendly shapes and strong silhouette reads.

### `ib-vector-cloth-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly dark fantasy cloth texture for awnings, banners, market shade, and hanging drapes; faded crimson, ochre, and indigo patch rhythm, sun-bleached wear, stitched repairs, broad folds suggested by flat polygon bands, no ornate brocade, no modern weave pattern, no text. Keep it simple and phone-readable.

### `ib-vector-plaster-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly ancient plaster and stucco texture for bath courts, shrine walls, patched retaining walls, and old garden façades; warm chalky mineral surface, cracks, chipped edge fields, repair patches, sun-burnt discoloration, no smooth modern stucco, no photoreal grime, no text. Flat vector geometry, broad readable plate shapes.

### `ib-vector-ceramic-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly ancient ceramic texture for cistern rims, amphorae, basins, shrine bowls, and mosaic insets; broken turquoise and white-blue glazed shards, earth clay edges, broad tile chips, no tiny bathroom mosaic grid, no modern kitchen tile, no text. Stylized flat vector shapes with clear material identity.

### `ib-vector-garden-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly hanging garden foliage texture for planter beds, vine mats, hanging greenery, terrace roots, and garden trays; dark olive, dusty green, and sun-burnt leaf clusters, creeping vine rhythm, no tropical jungle density, no photoreal grass, no flower wallpaper, no text. Broad flat shapes that read well on phone.

### `ib-vector-water-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly dark pool water texture for cisterns, runnels, baths, and shallow channels; green-blue mineral tint, subtle surface banding, murky reflective patches, no ocean waves, no stylized foam, no modern pool blue, no text. Keep it flat, restrained, and suitable for ancient waterworks.

### `ib-vector-rope-20260610.svg`
Seamless tileable low-poly rope texture for lash bindings, pulley lines, rope bridges, hanging baskets, and tethered bundles; broad twisted fiber rhythm, hemp and tar-dark variants, weathered wear, no cartoon spiral candy look, no ultra-detailed macro fiber, no text. Flat vector-friendly bands that still read as rope.

# Hanging Gardens Nook Story Pilot II

Date: 2026-06-11
Status: second contrasting district writing pilot

## Purpose

This second pilot contrasts the first Hanging Gardens pilot by moving from domestic intake/customs survival into shrine-rim witness culture.

Where the first pilot is about households hiding inside water and toll infrastructure, this one is about what remains when bells, rites, and route authority collapse together.

## Chosen Hidden Run Story State

This pilot uses:

- origin population: `chapel_custodians`
- secondary influence: `toll_remnant_scribes`
- rupture event: `belltower collapse and route severance`
- survival doctrine: `ritual substitution over doctrinal purity`
- taboo: `do not ring a bridge bell without witness`
- hope: `reopening the sealed cistern route`

## District Role

`shrine_rim_and_bell_sanctuary`

This district should read as:

- a high sanctuary and bell route after institutional collapse
- a place where law and rite still overlap, but only in fragments
- a refuge whose legitimacy now depends on witness counts, substitutions, and sealed-route hopes

## Contrast With Pilot I

Pilot I focused on:

- family concealment
- poisoned water memory
- quiet escape preparation
- dusk naming taboo

Pilot II focuses on:

- witness culture
- silent bells and failed warning systems
- substitute rites
- folded names and handprint law
- reopening a sealed cistern route as collective hope

## Repeated Motifs

- white plaster handprints as witness counts
- wrapped bell tongues kept like relics
- double civic/devotional seals
- upturned bowls for unblessed or tainted water
- vine-cut slit arrows toward the sealed cistern stair

## Route-Level Story Arc

If encountered in rough traversal order, this district story should unfold like this:

1. The bells cannot be trusted alone anymore.
2. Witness counts replace lost authority.
3. Law survives only if mercy rewrites it.
4. Water purity, ritual purity, and bodily safety have split apart.
5. The dead are preserved physically when they cannot be spoken openly.
6. Children inherit rhythm, count, and route before doctrine.
7. The sealed cistern route becomes the district's collective hope.
